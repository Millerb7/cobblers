# EXP-001: Curated Route

**Status: stub.** Objective and success criteria only; nothing is implemented. See `experiments/README.md` for the template to fill in when this experiment starts.

## Objective
Prove that we can author a route whose Pokemon availability is controlled by biome, coordinates, height, time of day, weather and campaign progression, so that an area has roughly 10-20 deliberate species and feels designed rather than randomly populated.

## Success criteria
- Encounters on the test route are limited to an authored list of 10-20 species; nothing else spawns there.
- At least one species is gated by each of: biome, coordinate region, height band, time of day, weather, and a progression flag.
- A player walking the route for 10 minutes meets a mix that matches the authored weights, and a playtester describes it as feeling authored.
- The authoring format is data (datapack / config), not code, and is checkable by tools/validate.py.
