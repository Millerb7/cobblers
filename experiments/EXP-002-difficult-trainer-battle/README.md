# EXP-002: Difficult Trainer Battle

**Status: stub.** Objective and success criteria only; nothing is implemented. See `experiments/README.md` for the template to fill in when this experiment starts.

## Objective
Prove that we can define a trainer whose team is fully specified (species, levels, moves, abilities, natures, held items, IVs/EVs) with competent AI including switching, and that rewards and rematches work in multiplayer.

## Success criteria
- A trainer definition sets species, level, moveset, ability, nature, held item, IVs and EVs for every team member and they are respected in battle.
- The AI switches Pokemon and picks moves well enough that a team of equal level does not win by spamming one move.
- Rewards (items, money, flags) are granted exactly once per player per win; rematches can be enabled or disabled.
- Two players can fight the same trainer independently without interfering with each other.
