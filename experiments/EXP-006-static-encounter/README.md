**Status: stub.** Objective and success criteria only; nothing is implemented. See `experiments/README.md` for the template to fill in when this experiment starts.

## Objective
Prove that a specific Pokemon with controlled species and level can be placed at a location as a one-time or repeatable encounter that is multiplayer-safe and yields a clear reward.

## Success criteria
- The encounter spawns the configured species at the configured level (and form/shiny/ability if specified) in the configured place.
- One-time and repeatable modes both work and are tracked per player.
- Two players cannot both take a one-time encounter, and neither can break the other's state.
- The reward (the Pokemon itself or an item) is unambiguous.
