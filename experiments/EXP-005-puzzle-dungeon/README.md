# EXP-005: Puzzle Dungeon

**Status: stub.** Objective and success criteria only; nothing is implemented. See `experiments/README.md` for the template to fill in when this experiment starts.

## Objective
Prove that a dungeon with at least three puzzle states, persistent progress, a sealed final chamber, one special Pokemon reward and one hidden optional reward can be built and survives server restarts.

## Success criteria
- Three distinct puzzle states are visible in-world and progress persists across relog and server restart.
- The final chamber cannot be entered (or the reward taken) until the puzzles are solved.
- Exactly one special Pokemon reward and one hidden optional reward exist and can each be claimed once per player.
- A second player arriving mid-puzzle sees a consistent state.
