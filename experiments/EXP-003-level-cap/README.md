# EXP-003: Level Cap

**Status: stub.** Objective and success criteria only; nothing is implemented. See `experiments/README.md` for the template to fill in when this experiment starts.

## Objective
Find a level-cap mechanism that stops grinding past a wall: evaluate hard cap, EXP suppression, badge-based cap, obedience, soft cap and a custom implementation, and choose one.

## Success criteria
- A capped Pokemon cannot exceed the cap by any EXP source (battles, candies, capture XP).
- The cap rises with progression (e.g. badges) without admin intervention.
- Players get clear feedback when they hit the cap.
- The chosen mechanism works per player in multiplayer and survives server restarts.
