# EXP-065: Smeargle, the seventh starter: three forms, Protean, and Sketch capped at 10

**Status: NOT RUN. BUILT, NOT OBSERVED IN GAME (2026-10-08).** Everything below is from code and the 1.8.0 jar.
Valid JSON and a JavaScript text that evaluates under Node after Cobblemon's newline stripping are the most that has
been shown.

## Objective

Show in a running server that the seventh starter works as decided (the owner, 2026-10-08; `docs/STATE.md`
"Smeargle, the seventh starter"):

1. the 30 and 45 steps are same-species form changes (`cobblers_starter_1` -> `_2` -> `_3`), each offered;
2. Protean is the ability in all three forms, kept across both steps;
3. Sketch copies a move for good and the copy stays when Sketch is benched;
4. the 11th Sketch fails with "But it failed!";
5. the count survives the PC, a trade and a restart;
6. a Spore + Shell Smash Smeargle against Sabrina and against Lorelei, before trusting the measure.

## Success criteria

Each step passes or fails on its own.

- Steps 1 and 2 decide whether the line stands as built. **Step 1 depends on EXP-049 step 3** (a same-species
  evolution moving form to form), which has never run; Smeargle needs it twice. If EXP-049 step 3 fails, this line
  fails with it, and the four other same-species lines do too.
- Steps 3 to 5 decide the Sketch cap. If step 4 fails, Sketch is unlimited (`smeargle-protean-sketch-1.8.0.md`
  section 2) and the owner decides whether to ship that.
- Step 6 is a balance reading, not a pass/fail: it says whether 450 is too strong once Sketch's utility is played.

## Dependencies

- Staging server and a staging world (never `cobblers-10240`), with `cobblers_mythical_starters` rebuilt
  (`python tools/mythical_starters.py build`) and installed. A restart after install: the move override reaches
  Showdown on a data load (`Moves.reload` -> `ShowdownService.sendRegistryData`).
- Two players for the trade in step 5.
- EXP-049 step 3 first, or run alongside it.

## Test instructions

Run on staging as an operator. Read each value from the summary screen or `/pokemonedit`; Mega Showdown's Stats-tab
"Dynamax Level" bar shows the count (`DynamaxLevelHandler` mirrors `dmaxLevel`).

1. **Forms.** Pick Smeargle on the starter screen. Expect level 5, form Starter, 54/58/44/58/44/72, ability
   Protean. Give it Rare Candy to 30 (with the cap raised for the test, or after the badges): the evolution is
   offered; accept it. Expect Starter-Grown, 71/76/57/75/57/94. Repeat at 45: Starter-Final, 74/79/60/79/60/98.
   Check that a wild Smeargle (`/pokespawn smeargle`) is the plain 250 species with Own Tempo, Technician or Moody.
2. **Protean.** At each form, the ability reads Protean. In a wild battle, use Flamethrower first: Smeargle turns
   Fire; use a Normal move next: it stays Fire (Gen 9, once per switch-in). Switch out and in: it can change again.
3. **Sketch copies and benches.** Against a wild Pokemon that has used a move, use Sketch. Expect
   the battle's Sketch line naming the copied move, the copy in Sketch's slot after the battle, and Sketch on the bench (re-slottable
   from the summary). Expect the chat line "Your Smeargle copied a move with Sketch (1 of 10)." and Dynamax Level 1.
4. **The cap.** Set the count to 9 (`/pokemonedit <slot> dmax_level=9`), Sketch once more (count 10, the chat says
   "From now on, Sketch will fail."), re-slot Sketch, and use it in the next battle. Expect "But it failed!", the
   moveset unchanged, and the count still 10. Also: a battle Sketch was not used in leaves the count alone; running
   from a battle after a Sketch counts it.
5. **The count survives.** With the count at a known value: put Smeargle in the PC and take it out; trade it to a
   second player; restart the server. Each time read the count again. Then have the second player Sketch once: the
   count goes up by one from where it was.
6. **The fight.** A level-capped Smeargle at 45-ish with Spore (Sketched from a Breloom or Parasect) and Shell Smash
   (from a Cloyster or Shuckle) against Sabrina, then at the cap against Lorelei. Record the result and how many
   Pokemon it took alone. No Sleep Clause applies (the research note section 2).

## Results

Not run.

What was checked without a game (2026-10-08):

- The jar claims the design rests on, re-read in `Cobblemon-fabric-1.8.0+1.21.1.jar` (snapshot 2026-10-05):
  `Moves.reload` lists `moves/*.js` in every namespace and keys each by its file name; `sendRegistryData` removes
  every `[\r\n]+`; `index.js` `receiveData` evals the joined text; `sim/dex-moves.js:173` asks
  `Cobblemon.registries.move` first; `PokemonProperties` carries `dmax_level` (match and apply);
  `Pokemon.setDmaxLevel` clamps to `maxDynamaxLevel`; `BattleRegistry.packTeam` writes the level, empty at 10;
  `sim/pokemon.js:118` clamps to 0..10; the three event names and their `players` context; the Molang names the
  callbacks use. Mega Showdown 1.0.2's patched `pokemon.js` keeps the clamp, its `index.js` keeps `receiveData`,
  its Sketch is the stock code, and it ships no `dex-moves.js`.
- The generated `sketch.js`, stripped of newlines and evaluated as `receiveData` does, under Node 20: Sketch copies
  at count 0 and 9 and returns false at 10, leaving the slot.
- `python tools/mythical_starters.py measure`: Smeargle 19/35 against the gym leaders, field 15-20 (9/35 without
  Protean). Not trusted until step 6.

## Limitations

- `battle_sim` models no Spore, Shell Smash, Protect or priority, and Protean only as one type per duel.
- The leak case: a battle that ends with no callback (`/stopbattle`, a crash) and Sketch re-slotted before the next
  battle goes uncounted.
- Not checked: whether Mega Showdown's Dynamax Candy can be obtained (it raises the count).
