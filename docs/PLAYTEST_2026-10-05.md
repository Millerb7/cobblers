# Play test, 2026-10-05: the owner's notes

The owner and one friend played staging-2026-10-01 on the build of PR #119 (head `14542876`). These are the owner's
closing notes, verbatim in substance, grouped by system. Findings recorded during the session are review items 84-89
(`docs/REVIEW_2026-10-02.md`).

## Owner's notes

### Starters and modes
1. Challenge mode needs implementing, with Oak changed for starters (Oak offers the choice; Normal vs Challenge is
   chosen there). Today every trainer runs its Normal team (`tools/challenge_guide.py` status box;
   `data/trainers.json` `generation_contract.runtime_mode_selection`).

### Direction and structure
2. Direct the player to the Mega pits after Koga.
3. Make the player visit side islands (or similar) so the game is not a straight gym-to-gym path.
4. Rework gyms to include trainers to fight; keep optional, challenging trainers along routes.
5. Give the Deep's cavern city a purpose -- perhaps where the player gets something.
6. Give the player a reason to build a house and settle down to craft.

### Economy and items
7. A TM seller after every few gyms, for options.
8. An item that randomises a Pokemon's ability ("a capsule"); sell Ability Capsules and those items.
9. The Poke Mart (Cobblemon's default) never sells more than the default few items: scale it with gyms.
10. A more direct way to unlock useful held items (Choice Band, Life Orb, ...); the Elite Four's mart sells many.
11. A token per gym (or similar) the player swaps for a held item.
12. A more direct way to EV train that skips the grind.
13. Mega "carry": a Mega Pokemon in the party without a Pokemon holding its stone; steep price (about three Mega
    Stones).

### Fixes
14. Remove the feather chests from the Articuno, Zapdos and Moltres towers -- the friend got a free Moltres.
15. Add an instanced-chest mod and make every chest per-player.
