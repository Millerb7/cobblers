Starters

The starter screen offers one category, Cobblers, with 8 Pokemon, each at level 5. This page shows what each becomes and when. Kubfu and Type: Null need items to evolve or change form: each one's are under its own heading.

  1. Cosmog
  2. Kubfu
  3. Type: Null
  4. Poipole
  5. Meltan
  6. Larvesta
  7. Smeargle
  8. Misdreavus
  9. What this page leaves out

Tiers. Every line has the same three tiers:

  • Tier 1, the form the starter screen gives you at level 5.
  • Tier 2, a stronger form at level 30. You can first reach it on the way to gym 3, holding 2 badges.
  • Tier 3, the final Pokemon at level 45. You can first reach it on the way to gym 6, holding 5 badges.

Tiers 1 and 2 are the same total for all eight, so the lines differ in how their stats are spread, not in how many. Tier 3 is the standard final Pokemon, except for Smeargle, which has no evolution: its tier 3 is a third form of its own. Your level cap is the next leader's ace (Trainer battles), which is why a tier waits for a badge. When your starter qualifies, the game offers the evolution; you can wait.

Abilities. Each line lists every tier's abilities and what they do, in the game's own words. One marked hidden is that form's hidden ability, not its usual one; where the hidden ability is the same as the usual one, it is listed once.

Silvally: the memories

Silvally takes the type of the memory it holds: give it one to hold and it becomes that type. There are 17, one for every type but Normal; holding none, it stays Normal.

How to get them: Earned in the story, once you hold 5 badges.

What this page leaves out

  • Movesets: check the summary screen in game.
  • Where anything is found in the world: an item that is not sold is "earned in the story", with no more said.
  • Any Pokemon not given by the starter screen: the tiers and evolutions here are the starters' own.
  • Flutter Mane, in the Misdreavus line, has no model in the client pack yet, so in game it shows as a placeholder doll until its model ships. Its stats and evolution are as this page says.
