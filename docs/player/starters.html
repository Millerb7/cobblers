Starters

The starter screen offers one category, Cobblers, with 5 Pokemon, each at level 5. This page shows what each becomes and when. Kubfu and Type: Null need items to evolve or change form: each one's are under its own heading.

  1. Cosmog
  2. Kubfu
  3. Type: Null
  4. Poipole
  5. Meltan
  6. What this page leaves out

Tiers. Every line has the same three tiers:

  • Tier 1, the form the starter screen gives you at level 5.
  • Tier 2, a stronger form at level 30. You can first reach it on the way to gym 3, holding 2 badges.
  • Tier 3, the final Pokemon at level 45. You can first reach it on the way to gym 6, holding 5 badges.

Tiers 1 and 2 are the same total for all five, so the lines differ in how their stats are spread, not in how many. Tier 3 is the standard final Pokemon. Your level cap is the next leader's ace (Trainer battles), which is why a tier waits for a badge. When your starter qualifies, the game offers the evolution; you can wait.

Kubfu: the evolution items

Which item you use decides what it becomes.

  • Scroll of Waters: Earned in the story, once you hold 5 badges.

Silvally: the memories

Silvally takes the type of the memory it holds: give it one to hold and it becomes that type. There are 17, one for every type but Normal; holding none, it stays Normal.

How to get them: Not on sale yet: the campaign prices it for a shop counter, but no merchant stocks it today.
