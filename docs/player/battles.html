Contents

Every trainer fight, in the order you meet them

90 trainers: each gym's route, its juniors and its leader, then Victory Road, the Elite Four and the Champion. The switch at the top changes every team on the page between Normal (in game: Kanto) and Challenge (in game: Kanto: Full-Team rules). Coordinates are block positions (x y z).

Level caps. Your Pokemon stop gaining experience at the cap. The cap is the strongest Pokemon of the next leader you have not beaten, plus 0 (rctmod relativeLevelCap); it is at least 20 from the start (initialLevelCap), and 100 after the Champion. A trainer refuses to battle a party over the cap. Each section shows the cap that holds until its leader falls.

Rules. Every trainer file here plays singles, uses no items in battle (maxItemUses 0, bag empty), does not heal you (healPlayers false) and does not scale anyone's levels (adjustPlayerLevels false, adjustNPCLevels false).

Battles on sight means the trainer starts the fight when it sees you within that distance; Talk to battle means you choose when.
