Fishing

Every area with water has its own catch: the Pokemon you see swimming there, and a few you can only get with a rod. 65 areas and seas, 132 species. Search by Pokemon or place.

How it works: cast a Poke Rod into water and wait for a bite. What bites depends on where your bobber lands, not where you stand. Rare and very rare Pokemon bite more often on a rod than you meet them on foot, so fishing is the best way to the rare end of an area. Shiny and alpha chances are Cobblemon's own. Bait on the rod is Cobblemon's own system too.

Dungeons

Some dungeons have a fishing room with a pool, a pier and a rod to borrow; what bites there depends on your band. These rooms are not catching yet: we are fixing them. Dungeon bait rooms are different: you set out a berry and the berry's rarity sets the odds that what comes is shiny or alpha (common berries 1% shiny and 5% alpha, up to 8% and 20% for the rarest).

What this page leaves out

  • Places outside our mapped areas: there, fishing is Cobblemon's default for the biome.
  • Exact chances: "How often" is the bite's rarity group; within a group some Pokemon are more common than others.
  • What happens in a running game: this is worked out from our data, not counted cast by cast. Where the game differs, the game is right: say so.
