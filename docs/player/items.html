Items and money

How you earn money, and every item you can buy, barter for, sell or craft. Pick a part, or search for an item by name.

How money works

Beating trainers

Money comes from beating trainers. Wild battles pay nothing. A win pays by the beaten team's levels added up: on average about 0.28 times the square of that total, and anywhere from two thirds to four thirds of the average.

The bank

Every shopkeeper at a counter, a market stall or a Poke Mart opens the bank: shift and right-click them, or press the Bank button on their shop screen. Put items in and press Sell; each is paid at the bank's price, the same in every town. What it buys is under Selling.

The Produce Buyer

The Produce Buyer at the apricorn farm buys farm goods by the crate, and pays less as your badges grow. A crate is one of:

  • 32 crops
  • 64 melon slices
  • 32 apricorns
  • 16 berries
  • 16 fish
  • 16 ranch goods
  • 16 meat
  • 64 dried kelp
  • 32 nether wart

Paid training

The coach at each training ground sells three shortcuts. Training by battle, or crafting the items (see Crafting), is always cheaper.

  • Train to your cap: $500 a Pokemon, raised straight to your level cap (up to a cap of 62).
  • Effort training: $17,200 a Pokemon, a two-stat training plan, once you hold the Cascade Badge.
  • Perfect a stat: $17,200 a stat, or $103,200 for all six, once you hold the Earth Badge.
