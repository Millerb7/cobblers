Nuzlocke tracker

89 catch zones in the order you meet them, grouped by region: 61 land, 20 sea, 6 cave, 1 waterway, 1 special place. In a Nuzlocke the first wild Pokemon you meet in each zone is your only catch there. After them come 13 dungeons, one encounter each whatever the level, and each zone lists the legendary Pokemon its ground holds (46 in all), with a box to tick for each.

Rules this page helps with. One catch per zone. A Pokemon that faints is gone. The dupes clause: if the first Pokemon you meet is from an evolution line you have already Caught or Failed, it is a dupe and you may meet another; the species list in each zone marks those for you. Your own Nuzlocke rules decide the rest: this page keeps score and enforces nothing.

What this page leaves out

  • Where a legendary is in its zone, what opens the way to it, its level or whether it can be caught: this page names the legendaries each zone holds and nothing else about them. Starters and gifts are not wild encounters and are not listed, and a dungeon's own boss or prize is part of that dungeon's one encounter, not a separate tick. A legendary that only an add-on of the base game places may be missing.
  • Anything else you find by exploring (hearts, elder trees, saplings and other discoveries): the species list in each zone is its ordinary wild Pokemon only. A catch from one of those places goes under "Other" in the species box.
  • The Nether and the trainers' own Pokemon: not catch zones here.
  • Which Pokemon you will meet first: the list is what can appear, not what will. Rod catches count as the zone's first encounter only if you decide your rules say so.
  • Zone borders: a zone is a place the game titles when you enter it; where the borders run is in-game, not drawn here. See the region map for the wild areas.
  • Whether it works in a running game: the zones come from our data, not from a play-through. Where the game differs, the game is right: say so.
