TMs

Every TM and when you can have it. Pick a badge to see what it opens, or search by move, type or kind (try "ground", "status" or "earthquake").

How you get a TM

There are 802 TMs. Each is listed under the badge that opens it, the first moment you can craft it. Every TM can be crafted, and that is the one way that never runs out; the other ways are shortcuts for a few.

  • Craft it. Hold the badge and the recipe appears in your crafting recipe book (most need a blank disc of the grade shown, plus the gems and items the recipe book lists). Before you hold the badge the recipe is not there. For 452 of the 802 TMs this is the only way: no shop, no leader and no machine offers them.
  • Buy it at a counter. 70 TMs are on sale now at a counter shopkeeper. A counter cannot check your badges, so the price is the gate: each is priced above the most money you can have earned by then (a first-badge TM costs more than the $4,033 you earn on the way to the first gym, a second-badge TM more than the $8,608 you have earned by the second; see How money works). The Poke Marts and the barter board sell no TM.
  • Win it from a gym leader. On your first win over each leader you receive one TM, drawn from that leader's list (23 TMs are on some leader's list). Rematches give none.
  • Print it at a TM Machine. Cobblemon's own machine (iron, tinted glass, copper and redstone; its blank TMs are two glass and two amethyst shards) prints a TM from gems and a few other items. It does not check badges here, so a player with the gems can print a TM before its badge. 333 of these TMs, and 2 more moves (below), are on its menu.

TMs by badge: gym 1 (the Boulder Badge): 164; gym 2 (the Cascade Badge): 120; gym 3 (the Thunder Badge): 99; gym 4 (the Rainbow Badge): 85; gym 5 (the Soul Badge): 124; gym 6 (the Marsh Badge): 99; gym 7 (the Volcano Badge): 77; gym 8 (the Earth Badge): 34. Cobblemon marks some machine moves as unlocked through play; whether this server holds the machine to that has not been run.
