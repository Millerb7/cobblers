What wild Pokemon drop

Defeat a wild Pokemon and it may drop items. Here is what each of the 607 Pokemon you can meet in the wild drops, and which Pokemon to look for when you want an item. Search by Pokemon or item.

How to read it: "Bone (1-2): about 1 in 4 defeats" means that in about one defeat in four you get one or two Bones. Each defeat is rolled on its own, and a Pokemon can drop several things at once. Drops fall to the ground where the Pokemon was.
