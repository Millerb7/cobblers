Challenge mode

Full-Team rules: what you are offered at the start of your journey, what it gives you, and how it differs from Standard.

What Challenge mode is

The game calls it Kanto: Full-Team rules: "Every Gym Leader brings six. The level limits are the ones the Standard rules use." Standard is the ordinary rules, and Challenge is the one other choice you are offered at the start of your journey.

  • The level limits are the same in both modes. Only the battles change.
  • The 13 bosses (the 8 Gym Leaders, the Elite Four and the Champion): Standard teams run from 3 to 6 Pokemon; Challenge teams are all 6. Across the 13, 65 Pokemon carry a held item in Standard and 77 in Challenge.
  • The choice is yours alone and is written down once: it stays. It is offered at the start of your journey, and not to a player who already holds the first badge.
  • Challenge also pays a sum of cash and gives a few TMs when you choose it and with each badge (below), and a TM Case to keep them in.

Every team in both modes, boss by boss, is on Trainer battles: its switch at the top flips the whole page between Standard (shown as Normal there) and Challenge.

The cash

Each payment is made once, as you first earn it: the first when you choose Challenge, the rest the first time you win each badge. Together they come to $760,800.

The TMs

A Challenge player is given TMs that answer the next boss, once, at the same moments as the cash. They are on top of the cash and do not come out of it. To use one, see TMs; whether your Pokemon can learn a given move is for you to check.

The TM Case

When you choose Challenge you are also given a TM Case: an ordinary backpack with a new name, with room for any item, TMs among them. It does not sort anything for you. If your inventory is full, what does not fit is dropped at your feet.

Against Standard

  • The same level limits, badges, gyms and routes.
  • The bosses: Standard teams hold 3 to 6 Pokemon, Challenge teams 6, with 77 held items against 65. The Gym Leaders alone: Standard 3 to 6 Pokemon, Challenge 6.
  • Challenge pays the cash and gives the TMs and the TM Case above. Standard does not.
  • Route trainers have a Challenge team as well as a Standard one; both are on Trainer battles.

What this page leaves out

  • The conversation in which the choice is offered, and anything anyone says.
  • How it plays in a running game. The cash and the gifts are worked out from the campaign's data and have not been watched being given; where the game differs, the game is right: say so.
