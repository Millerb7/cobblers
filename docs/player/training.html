Training coaches

Eight coaches will raise a Pokemon to your level cap, train its effort or perfect its stats, for a price that rises with your badges.

The eight coaches

One coach stands at the training ground of each gym town. Coordinates are block positions (x y z). The coach's greeting: "Want a shortcut? I train a Pokemon to your level cap, train in its effort, or perfect its stats. My prices rise with your badges."

What the coaches do

Each coach sells three services. You pay in CobbleDollars, the coach asks which Pokemon (slot 1 to 6 of your party), and nothing is charged if the request cannot be done. Prices rise with your badges; see What it costs.

  • Train a Pokemon to your cap. One Pokemon is raised straight to your current level cap and no higher. It will not train one already at or above the cap, and it stops once your cap is above 62. Moves it would have learned on the way are worth checking afterwards.
  • Effort training. The Pokemon's effort values are cleared and one of these plans trained in:
    • Fast attacker: 252 Attack, 252 Speed, 4 HP
    • Fast special attacker: 252 Sp. Atk, 252 Speed, 4 HP
    • Bulky attacker: 252 HP, 252 Attack, 4 Speed
    • Bulky special attacker: 252 HP, 252 Sp. Atk, 4 Speed
    • Physical wall: 252 HP, 252 Defence, 4 Sp. Def
    • Special wall: 252 HP, 252 Sp. Def, 4 Defence
    One price a Pokemon, whichever plan.
  • Perfect a stat. One stat of a Pokemon set to 31 (the best a stat can be), or all six for six times the price. A stat already at 31 costs the same again.

A Pokemon above your badges' level is priced as the badge count its level needs, for effort training and for perfecting a stat. A raise is always priced by the badges you hold. The cap a raise writes at each badge count: no badge: 20; 1 badge: 25; 2 badges: 30; 3 badges: 35; 4 badges: 40; 5 badges: 45; 6 badges: 50; 7 badges: 55; 8 badges: 60.

Worked examples

One Pokemon, with 1 badge. Raised to your cap of 25: $200. Its effort trained: $400. All six stats perfected: $600. All three together: $1,200.

A full team of six, with 5 badges. Six raises to your cap of 45 at $1,100 each: $6,600. Six effort plans at $3,300 each: $19,800. Thirty-six stats perfected at $600 each: $21,600. All of it: $48,000.

These are the prices above multiplied out; the coaches charge them one purchase at a time.

What this page leaves out

  • How the services play in a running game. The data says they are built and have not been run in game yet; the prices are read from it. Where the game differs, the game is right: say so.
  • What happens to a Pokemon's moves or evolution after a raise: not checked.
  • Once your cap is past 62 (after the Champion): a raise is refused, and the other two services stay at the eight-badge price.
