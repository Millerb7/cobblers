# Structure inventory and classification

**Status: Step 1 of the placement work, for review.** Nothing is placed. The strategy is
decided: every overworld structure is hand-placed inside the authored map, and no content
depends on worldgen scatter. This document is the inventory that decision needs.

> **Amended 2026-09-13.** Decisions and experiment results since this report are in
> [`STRUCTURE_DECISIONS.md`](STRUCTURE_DECISIONS.md):
> - **The Nether and the End stay vanilla-generated dimensions.** Blaine, the Kanto League,
>   Moltres, the four Ruin shrines, Eternatus and both Necrozma towers are no longer
>   relocation candidates.
> - **The overworld border is 10240** ([`DIMENSIONS_AND_BORDERS.md`](DIMENSIONS_AND_BORDERS.md)).
> - **EXP-013 answered the placement-method question:** no method writes structure data.
>
> The sections below are corrected where they said otherwise.

The data behind this report:

| File | What it is | How made |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `data/structures.json` | Every structure, with its authored class and disposition | proposed schema `cobblers.structures/1` |
| [`STRUCTURE_INVENTORY_TABLE.md`](STRUCTURE_INVENTORY_TABLE.md) | One row per structure | generated |
| `derived/structures/inventory.json` | The full measurement | generated by `tools/structure_inventory.py` |

The tool reads the server's real files, the same way `tools/spawn_biomes.py` does: the
vanilla 1.21.1 jar, every mod jar with its nested jars, and the datapacks the world has
enabled.

For each structure it records:
- its source and type;
- its biome requirement, resolved to loaded biomes;
- its structure set and spacing;
- its footprint, read from template NBT;
- what its templates contain: loot tables, trainer spawners, altars, command blocks,
  spawners, entities;
- what depends on it: spawn entries, advancements, and loot tables, trades or recipes that
  name it.

It also scans the saved world's region files.

## b. What exists in the exported world, and why

The world scanned is the live server's `erosion-land-8k`. It was exported from the rejected
heightmap, but through the same WorldPainter pipeline, so the finding carries over.

| Measure | Inside 0-8191 | Outside |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Chunks | 262,144 | 5,901 |
| Chunk status | `full` 238,694 (as WorldPainter wrote them), `minecraft:full` 23,450 (re-saved by the server) | from `structure_starts` to `full`; 3,088 still at `structure_starts` |
| Chunks carrying a `structures` compound | 23,450, all empty | 5,901 |
| Structure starts | **0** | **62** |

**Why nothing is inside:**
- The generation settings did not suppress structures. `level.dat` has
  `generate_features: 1`, the overworld generator is `minecraft:noise` with the vanilla
  overworld settings, and `server.properties` has `generate-structures=true`.
- WorldPainter writes every chunk as already fully generated, with no structure data.
  Minecraft only places structures and features while generating a chunk, and a `full` chunk
  is never generated again.
- So the export suppressed structures **implicitly, through chunk status, not through a
  setting**.

**Outside the map, worldgen is still running.** There is no world border (`BorderSize` is
the default 59,999,968). Chunks generated beyond the export by Distant Horizons pregen and
exploration already hold 62 structure starts:

| Structure | Starts |
| --- | ---: |
| Mineshafts (vanilla 21, RS desert 4, birch 1, ocean 1) | 27 |
| Mega Showdown mega sites | 4 |
| **Brock's gym** | **2** |

### How hand placement behaves

The method used to paste a structure decides what still works. Sources and experiments are
in `docs/research/notes/hand-placed-structures.md`.

| What depends on structure data | Needs a structure start or reference in the chunk | On a hand-placed map | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cobblemon `structures` spawn conditions: villages, ruins, coves, trail ruins, mansions… | yes. Cobblemon 1.8.0 reads the structure starts of the spawn position's chunk | **never pass** in exported chunks unless placement writes starts | mechanism verified in source; outcome inferred |
| RCT trainer spawners: every gym leader, the Elite Four, Rocket grunts | no. The block entity and its `TrainerIds` are enough | should work however the template is pasted | source verified; spawning untested |
| Cobbleverse `gym_map` | yes. It is a vanilla `minecraft:exploration_map` function aimed at `cobbleverse:kanto_brock_gym`. I read the loot table; vanilla skips already-generated chunks by default | **will not find** a hand-placed gym | loot table read; outcome inferred |
| LumyMon cartography maps, gym locator items, legendary radars | not public: LumyMon is closed source | probably do not find hand-placed builds | not verified |
| `/locate`, explorer maps, `location_check` advancements (Mega Showdown "find" advancements) | yes | will not match | mechanism verified |
| Command blocks inside templates (Brock's map guide, Mew's temple, the Necrozma towers) | no, but `enable-command-block=true` is needed | Brock's run only from their own trigger, never on placement (EXP-013 C) | observed for Brock |

**What paste methods leave behind** (EXP-013, run headless on a disposable world):
- **No structure data from any command.** `/place structure`, `/place jigsaw` and
  `/place template` all left every chunk with empty `starts` and `References`. `/locate`,
  `location_check` and the gym map ignored the builds; natural controls passed.
- **Structure blocks, WorldEdit and Axiom** store only blocks, block entities and entities.
  They were not run.
- **`/place structure` misplaces jigsaws on painted terrain.** It placed village houses and
  Brock's gym at seed-terrain height, 20–54 blocks under the painted surface.
  `/place jigsaw` dropped the houses. `/place template` at an explicit Y lands where asked.

**Consequences:**
- Every structure-gated spawn and every locator item in the pack stops working for
  overworld builds. Only the Nether and End keep them, because they generate normally.
- The replacements are Habitat Blocks, non-structure spawn conditions, and authored
  guidance instead of locator maps.
- EXP-013 D (spawns) and E (trainer spawning) still need a player; see
  `STRUCTURE_DECISIONS.md`.

## c. Classification

**The rules** (recorded per structure in `data/structures.json` with a one-line reason):

- **PROGRESSION**: the nine Kanto gym and league structures. The RCT Kanto series and
  CobbleverseBadges key off their leaders, and the league elevator is advancement-gated.
- **LEGENDARY**: every structure whose template carries a legendary encounter, whether an
  altar, shrine, summon anchor or statue, a scripted `pokespawnat`, or legendary trial
  spawners. That is all Cobbleverse `legendary/` and `mythical/` sites, the crown sites, the
  Necrozma towers, and every Legendary Monuments structure except the outskirt stand.
- **NAMED**:
  - one-off dungeons, fortifications and settlements;
  - Pokémon set pieces off the badge path (Ash's house, the Rocket tower);
  - sites holding a unique mechanic item (Key Stone megaroid, observatory, wishing weald,
    archaeological site);
  - the three shipwreck coves and three henges, which carry their own treasure or spawns.
- **SCATTER**: everything else. These are repeatable by nature. Mega sites sit here too:
  they are common by design, even though each holds a reward.

| Class | Overworld IDs | Families | Outside the overworld | Never generate |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- | ---: |
| PROGRESSION | 7 | 7 | Blaine (Nether), Kanto League (End) | |
| LEGENDARY | 5 | 5 | Moltres and 4 Ruinous shrines (Nether); Eternatus, Dawn and Dusk towers (End) | 7, including Giratina island, which generates only in the Distortion World dimension |
| NAMED | 71 | 27 | 13 Nether, 3 End (fortresses, bastions, end cities, RS variants) | |
| SCATTER | 116 | 21 | 17 Nether, 5 End | |

**How placement rules will differ by class**, carried into Step 3:
- PROGRESSION must sit on the critical path.
- LEGENDARY may be hidden but needs a distance cue.
- NAMED rewards curiosity.
- SCATTER fills space.
- The out-of-overworld PROGRESSION and LEGENDARY structures are not placed. They generate in
  their own dimensions and are audited there.

## e. Scale of the authoring job

Three readings, in overworld placements. The worldgen-equivalent counts model each structure
set on this map:
- one attempt per spacing cell, reduced by the set's frequency;
- succeeding in proportion to the area of planned biomes the structure accepts.

They ignore exclusion zones and terrain checks, so they are upper estimates of what vanilla
generation would have put here.

| Class | One of every ID | One of every family | Every family in every region it fits | Worldgen density |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| PROGRESSION (7 overworld; Blaine and the League stay in their dimensions) | 7 | 7 | 7 | ~28 duplicates, meaningless for unique gyms |
| LEGENDARY (5 overworld + 5 never-generating that can be hand-placed; 8 stay in the Nether and End) | 10 | 10 | 10 | ~1 |
| NAMED | 71 | 27 | 27 | ~525 (trial chambers alone ~227, megaroids ~91) |
| SCATTER | 116 | 21 | **153** | **~3,530** (mineshafts ~1,250, Cobblemon ruins ~990, habitats ~233, ocean ruins ~202, ruined portals ~164, fishing boats ~161, shipwrecks ~154, mega sites ~128) |
| **Total** (as first reported, with the 10 out-of-overworld sites; 204 / 65 / 197 without them) | **214** | **75** | **207** | **~4,100** |

On top of that sit the worldgen features (ore veins, fossils, apricorn trees, berries,
plants, waystones), which are not counted here and run to thousands of individual
placements at vanilla density.

**What the numbers mean:**
- **Unique content is about 75-100 hand placements:** PROGRESSION, LEGENDARY, and one NAMED
  place per family.
- **Scatter decides the size of the job.** Between one of each family per suitable region
  (about 150) and worldgen density (about 3,500) there is a factor of 23.
- **Two scatter families are really spawn and economy systems, not places:**
  - Cobblemon habitats are spawn points.
  - Ruins feed Gimmighoul and relic coins.
  - How many of these to place is a spawn-design question tied to
    `docs/mechanics/SPAWN_PHILOSOPHY.md`.

## Findings along the way

1. **References to structures that do not exist in the loaded pack:**
   - Cobblemon's mansion presets list both `minecraft:mansion` and the pre-1.18 ID
     `minecraft:woodland_mansion`. That reads as a compatibility alias and does no harm, since
     the real ID is also listed.
   - Two entries need `minecraft:desert_well`, which is a feature, not a structure, so that
     condition can never pass.
   - Galarian Articuno, Zapdos and Moltres need `cobbleverse:dyna_tree`, and Raikou, Entei and
     Suicune need `cobbleverse:burned_tower`. Both exist only in disabled packs.
