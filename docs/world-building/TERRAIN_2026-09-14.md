lowest ground inside each outline. Transects are spaced 32–48 blocks apart, and levels are
reported against y62.

## 4. Rivers: none of them holds water

> **Followed up in [RIVERS.md](RIVERS.md).** The rivers were graded by descent rather than by
> repairing these carves. Every lake has a real outflow. Viltri's Path, the Peak Pond creek, the
> Marsh–Tilpey creek and the south-east branch cannot be rivers where drawn. The lake outflows
> and the channel south-west of Tilpey are cut into `land_8k_16_eroded_rivers.png`, not yet
> imported.

**Method:**
1. A guide line was drawn along each channel on a local-relief map: height minus its 100-block
   mean, which shows every carve.
2. A least-cost path then follows the most incised ground within 48 blocks of that guide.
3. **The drainage test:** water released at the higher end reaches the lower end only if the
   bed never rises more than 1 block above its running minimum.

| Channel | Annotated | Length | Incision (median) | Bed y | Worst rise | Drains end to end | Reaches the sea |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Viltri's Path (Lake Viltri to the west coast) | yes | 1,942 | 3.3 | 65–134 | **59 blocks** at (870, 3846) | no | no: the low end is at y65, a few blocks inland of the sea |
| River of Shrews (Shrew Lake south-west) | yes | 1,412 | 2.9 | 61–109 | 11 at (2218, 4602) | no | no: ends in a closed basin (spill y109) |
| Creek into Peak Pond | no | 931 | 3.3 | 88–134 | 29 at (3542, 1002) | no | no (ends in the pond) |
| Creek Marshy Marsh to Tilpey | no | 1,981 | 1.8 | 63–132 | 49 at (5774, 2562) | no | no |
| Creek south of Arrow Lake | no | 2,236 | 2.2 | 57–101 | 33 at (2930, 4870) | no | no |
| Its south-east branch | no | 1,319 | 2.3 | 75–114 | 34 at (3838, 5946) | no | no |
| Channel south-west of Tilpey | no | 479 | 3.2 | 70–118 | 7 at (5014, 4862) | no | no |

**Cross-sections agree:**
- Most transects on the rivers classify as `none`: under the minimum depth.
- The rest are shallow V or U grooves, 30–45 blocks wide at the floor and 100–190 blocks rim
  to rim.

**What the carves are:** grooves 2–6 blocks deep, pressed into the surface and following it
over hills. They are not channels graded downhill.
- **Viltri's Path** climbs 59 blocks over the western uplands between the lake and the coast.
- **The River of Shrews** is perched 40 blocks above Shrew Lake's floor. It drains away from
  the lake into an inland hollow.
- **Water placed in any of them** would sit in disconnected puddles or pour off the high
  points.

**Where natural drainage actually goes** (priority flood and D8 flow on the whole map):
- The glacier trough into Tilpey, which is closed at y77.9.
- Otherwise, many short radial lines from the uplands to the coast.
- None of the carved river lines is a natural drainage path.

**In the painted export the rivers are dry creek beds**: gravel, no trees.

**To make them rivers, one of these is needed:**
1. **Grade each bed in the heightmap:** a monotone profile to sea level through the high
   ground (cuts of up to ~60 blocks on Viltri's Path).
2. **Accept stepped water:** a series of ponds and falls, each its own water level. Not
   buildable through the high points without carving.
3. **Keep them as dry ravines** and name them so.

The choice changes how the western and central-south routes read, so it is left to you.
