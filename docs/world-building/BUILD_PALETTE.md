# Regional building palette

Every listed provider is world-critical once its blocks enter a campaign asset.
Confidence describes whether it belongs in the likely permanent palette; it is
not a claim that every block has been visually inspected on Cobblemon 1.8.

| Provider | Scale | Main visual purpose | Best campaign use | Keep confidence |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- | --- |
| Rechiseled | 3,628 blockstates | architectural material variants | town identity, façades, ruins, industrial detail | HIGH |
| Carved Wood | 325 | timber trim and carved variants | rural, forest, mountain and historic towns | HIGH |
| VanillaBackport | 72 + worldgen | newer vanilla palettes and sulfur-cave content | natural accents and caves | MEDIUM; worldgen test needed |
| CobbleFurnies | 372 | Pokémon labs, Centers and themed furniture | service buildings, labs, interiors | HIGH; upstream structures require it |
| Handcrafted | 268 | furniture and trim | homes, inns, shops, civic interiors | HIGH |
| Cozy Home | 278 | domestic furniture | houses, apartments, inns | HIGH |
| Moar Concrete | 178 | colored construction variants | cities, laboratories, villain facilities | HIGH |
| Cobblemon | native | PCs, healers and Pokémon-specific blocks | all functional Pokémon infrastructure | REQUIRED |
| Cobblemon Additions | 248 templates + blocks | settlement vocabulary and Pokémon villages | town kit, roads, markets and services | HIGH pending client inspection |
| Pokéblocks | 314 | figurines, dolls, baskets and themed props | shops, bedrooms, plazas, rewards | HIGH |
| LumyMon | 64 + mechanics | altars, statues and shrine blocks | legendary/story references | LOW until license/function tests |
| Beautify | 57 | lamps, blinds, trellis and frames | streets, gardens, shopfronts | HIGH |
| Legendary Monuments | 48 + structures | legendary monument blocks | ancient landmarks and reference palettes | LOW until license/function tests |
| Waystones | 45 | travel stones and village anchors | controlled regional fast travel | MEDIUM; progression implications |
| Comforts | 34 | sleeping bags and hammocks | camps and route rest areas | MEDIUM; healing/rest rules may restrict use |

Freeze exact versions before committing large structure variants. The dependency
manifest records each campaign asset's actual subset; do not make every palette
mod a dependency of every building.
