# Standard town kit

Reuse components, not identical layouts. Terrain, street shape, palette, local
landmark, encounter access, and side paths should make each settlement distinct.

## Small town

- 1 small or regionalized Pokémon Center.
- 1 Poké Mart or combined service shop.
- 4–8 houses or local businesses.
- 1 plaza or gathering space and 1 local landmark.
- 1 primary route connection and 1 optional side path.
- Optional small battle pad, camp, or environmental story scene.

## Medium town

- Regionalized Center and Mart.
- 8–15 buildings using two or three repeated component families.
- Gym exterior or major civic building where progression calls for one.
- Local landmark, multiple streets, side content, and at least two route links.
- One service or reward that gives the town a gameplay identity.

## Major city

Use districts rather than a fixed building count: arrival/service district,
commercial or civic core, residential edge, progression landmark, and optional
back route. Reuse BCA service and road pieces selectively; major skyline,
interiors, villain spaces, and story routes remain authored.

## Composition rules

1. Fit terrain and road hierarchy before placing buildings.
2. Reuse a recognizable Pokémon-service silhouette, then regionalize materials,
   landscaping, roofline and street furniture.
3. Preserve functional blocks and document their dependencies before export.
4. Repeat a component only when local palette and placement make it feel native.
5. Keep Gym challenge spaces and story landmarks distinct from the town kit.
