## Shrew Lake tea grower

Park a lived-in tea-growing smallholding at Shrew Lake, connected plausibly to Tea Town and composed of a house, drying racks, working terraces, and a path to the better lake water; three separate cherry-grove clearings each hold one sapling whose bird encounter is gated by its own weather rule, while the grower only says that years of watching have revealed a pattern and never states the solution. Its precise site, sapling coordinates, grower's identity, and Tea Town relationship remain deferred until the blocked rebuild paths permit export work.
