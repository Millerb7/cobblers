# Nuzlocke catch zones and their titles

The owner, 2026-10-06: *"Finish determining the regions a Nuzlocke player uses as catch zones, and make the location
titles pop up in game so a player knows when they have entered a new one. The location-title mechanism exists.
Connect it."*

- Data: `data/nuzlocke_zones.json` (generated by `tools/nuzlocke_zones.py`; never hand-edited)
- Titles: `build/datapacks/cobblers_titles` (generated by `tools/location_titles.py`)
- Tests: `tests/test_nuzlocke_zones.py`, `tests/test_location_titles.py`, `tests/test_location_titles_play.py`
- The map page that picks 4-6 encounters per gym from these areas: `tools/nuzlocke_map.py` (unchanged)

## The rule: one zone per encounter table

A Nuzlocke player takes the first encounter in each area. An **area is one encounter table**, because a table is
what makes one place's encounters differ from the next. The first encounter in a zone is the catch there, whether it
comes from the zone's table or from a site inside it (an elder tree, a sapling, a heart).

**A route leg is not a zone.** Measured 2026-10-06 from the data, and again from the compiled spawn pack:

- `data/encounter_design.json` has 63 tables and `data/regions.json` 63 sub-regions, the same ids.
- Every species a route corridor carries (`data/spawns.json` `route_species_selection`) is in the table of a
  sub-region that leg crosses: 0 exceptions on all 9 legs. In the compiled pack every route entry is tagged with its
  sub-region, and across the 39 (leg, sub-region) pairs no corridor species is outside that sub-region's own table.
- So a leg crosses 2 to 7 tables (Route 2 crosses 2, Route 4 crosses 7). Calling a leg one zone would give a player
  one catch where the game offers up to seven different tables; calling each sub-region off a leg a zone of its own,
  as well as the leg, would count one table twice.

- **Jungle West and Jungle East.** No 32-block raster cell of either has ground at or above y62: the Jungle Isle is
  under the sea. Their land tables still compile (defect 1). The test checks every 4th column of both polygons on the
  heightmap, not the generator's own raster.
- **16 Habitat Block pools with no placed block** (`displaced_city_cavern`, `rift_depths`, `tree_town_canopy`,
  `great_crater_bowls`, `marshy_marsh_basin`, `mining_town_fossil_levels`, `northgate_old_growth_grove`,
  `glacial_tear_deep_valley`, and eight `planned` sites such as `driftmouth_isle_waters`). Not in the world. A pool
  becomes a zone the day a block is placed: the generator reads `status`.
- **67 elder-tree and sapling pools.** A few blocks inside a sub-region: an encounter there is that sub-region's.
  Each is listed under its zone's `sites`, assigned by where its blocks stand, not by its name (the four Jungle Isle
  elders now stand on the Long Isle).
- **Hearts** (`<area>_h<n>`): a rarer spot inside an area, never an area (ENCOUNTER_DESIGN.md section 10).

### For the owner

- **The Windward Sea is three zones**, one per band, because each band is its own roster and level cap (16-20, 20-25,
  25-30). Making it one zone means one catch for the whole sea; say so and the generator merges them.
- **The Wooper creek is its own zone** inside the land zones it runs through: its table replaces the sub-region's
  along a 16-block strip. A stricter reading would fold it into its sub-region.

## The titles

`cobblers_titles` titles every land, sea, waterway and cave zone and every settlement (28). Each place is a pair of
hidden advancements: `in_<key>` (a `minecraft:location` trigger inside its boxes, reward: the title) and `out_<key>`
(outside the boxes grown by a margin, reward: re-arm). Checked against the 1.21.1 jar (`df.class`,
`data/minecraft/advancement/adventure/adventuring_time.json`): the `player` list of `minecraft:entity_properties`
conditions, `location.position` x/y/z ranges and `location.can_see_sky` are the 1.21.1 forms; folders are the 1.21
singular ones (`advancement`, `function`, `predicate`, `tags/function`); pack_format 48; text components are JSON.

### Why no title had been seen (found offline, 2026-10-06)

Not the syntax, not the install. The pack was installed in the server's global `datapacks/` (the 2026-10-05 server
snapshot's copy is byte-identical to `build/`), which Global Packs loads as required (`config/global_packs.toml`),
and `advancementdisable` disables only `beautify`, `rctmod` and `repurposed_structures` (its mixin matches the
advancement id's namespace exactly). Two logic faults could keep a player from ever seeing one:

1. **A region's title was granted silently inside a settlement and then held.** The enter function skipped the title
   when the player stood in a settlement but kept the advancement, so a player who started in Pallet never saw
   "Pallet Fields" on walking out: the pack thought it had already said it.
2. **Advancements persist in the player's file.** A player who logged out inside a place held its `in_` forever, so
   on every later login nothing fired until they crossed into somewhere else; and the first title of a first join
   fires in the player's first second, under the loading screen.

The play test reproduces fault 1: with the fixes mutated out of the generator, spawning in Pallet and walking into
Pallet Meadows shows nothing.

### How it behaves now

1. Inside a settlement or a Victory Road cave, a zone title is not shown and its `in_` is revoked again (retry), so it
   shows on stepping out.
2. Cooldown gate (`function cobblers:titles/gate`, game time, no tick): the title the screen last showed, shown under
   600 ticks ago, is held; the one before it (A, B, A on a border) is retried until its 600 ticks are up. At most one
   title per place per 30 seconds, and the screen ends up naming where the player is.
3. Re-arm on joining: a tick function sees a join (first ever, or `minecraft.leave_game` counted since) and 100 ticks
   later revokes every `in_` and forgets the cooldown; the place the player stands in titles at the next check.
4. Leaving a place (a real leave: `in_` held) re-arms every place, so stepping out of the creek or a town back into
   the zone around it titles that zone again, through the gate.
5. Hysteresis: a place is left 8 blocks past its edge (4 for a cave box).

Cost: the location trigger already tests every advancement a player does not hold once a second; the new pieces are
three selector passes per tick and a gate function per title.

### Install and re-application

- **Global, as now. It need not move.** It changes nothing in a world except advancements and its own `cob_t_*`
  scores, and the titles are wanted in every world on this map. It now has a load and a tick function; if the
  install policy is that every pack with a tick is world-local (`reapply.py` `WORLD_LOCAL`), move it there instead.
- **Steps** (`tools/reapply.py` is not edited here): `prepare` already runs `location_titles.py`; it should run
  `python tools/nuzlocke_zones.py --check` before it, so a stale zones file fails closed. `EXCLUDED`'s note for
  `cobblers_titles` ("enter_place_*, not applied to the world") should also name `enter_zone_*` and the tick.
  `install` copies it; nothing to apply. After install: `/reload` or a restart, then walk out of Pallet.

## What an audit must check

1. **The zone set is the table set.** Every `data/encounter_design.json` table is a land zone or a named exclusion;
   every compiled area in `build/datapacks/cobblers_spawns` (sub-region, marine, waterway file) is a zone or a named
   exclusion, and every zone has a compiled file (`python tools/nuzlocke_zones.py audit`, which reads the pack through
   `tools/nuzlocke_map.py`'s reader, not the generator).
2. **A leg is still not a table.** Recompute from `data/spawns.json`: every corridor species is in a crossed
   sub-region's table. The day one is not, the rule breaks and the generator stops.
3. **An excluded sub-region is really under water**, checked column by column on the heightmap, not on the raster
   the generator used.
4. **The titles title exactly the zones.** Every titled zone and settlement has one `in_`/`out_` pair, a name and a
   subtitle; no `region_*` key survives; a site zone's settlement is titled.
5. **The pack's logic, run, not read.** `tests/test_location_titles_play.py` walks a player through the built pack:
   spawn in Pallet (nothing for 5 s, then the town, then the meadows outside, then quiet), two minutes on a border
   (at most 10 titles, ending on the right name), a detour into the creek (the surrounding zone comes back), a
   re-login (titled again), a cave tile (titled only under no sky). Each of rules 2, 3 and 4 was mutated out of the
   GENERATOR and a test failed; rule 1 alone is covered twice (by 1 and by 4), so it must be mutated with rule 4.
6. **In game, which no test does:** the server log names no rejected advancement or function in `cobblers_titles`;
   a player sees "Pallet Town" about 5 s after joining there, "Pallet Meadows" on walking out, "Maze Forest" at the
   Route 1 transition (1468, 5059), and "The Bloom" inside Victory Road's Bloom; and relogging shows the place again.

## Defects recorded, not chased

1. Jungle West and Jungle East still compile land tables over open sea; the sea there has no marine table either.
2. 1,513 land cells (32-block cells whose centre is at or above y62) lie in no sub-region raster: seams between
   polygons and coastal fringes. No zone, no title and no sub-region table there (what spawns there is not measured).
   The Wooper creek crosses one at (1295, 1943).
3. A Habitat Block's influence is taken as a cube of `range_of_influence` round the block (ASSUMED; not read from
   the Cobblemon jar). Victory Road's tiles cover 89% of its floor (`derived/vr_caves/plan.json`), so in the rest a
   rift surface zone can title underground.
4. Eight pools are authored in `data/spawns.json` with no record at all in `data/habitat_blocks.json`.
5. Not verified in game: any of it.
