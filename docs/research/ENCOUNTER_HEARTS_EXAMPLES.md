# Encounter hearts: eight places as a player meets them (2026-10-02)

For the owner, to judge the hearts (`docs/mechanics/ENCOUNTER_DESIGN.md` section 10) before the numbers are
confirmed. Every figure is from the compiled pack (`tools/compile_spawns.py`, then `tools/encounter_audit.py`):
a chance is the share of that context's spawns (land or water) under the audit's rule, with Cobbleverse's bucket
weights renormalised over the buckets present and every level in a range taken as equally likely. Night-only and
rain-only spawns are counted at all times. **Nothing here has been seen in game.**

"Outskirts" means the place's base table: everywhere outside the heart, including the whole band within 128 blocks
of the path. "Inside the heart" means the base table plus the heart's own spawns.

## The south-west fields (tier 1, cap 20, band 6-14; off the path, 337 blocks from Route 1)

The outskirts are a first meadow: Cottonee (44%), Pikachu and Growlithe (22% each), Nidoran female and male (5% each),
all 6-14, and an Eevee (1.2%, 10-14) for whoever looks. The heart is an 80-block circle in the middle of the fields,
10% of their area, 465 blocks from the nearest route. Every outskirts spawn is still there, and grown Nidorina and
Nidorino (1.7% each, 16-25) wander among them: a level-20 player can catch the ones at 16-20 and will see bigger
ones to come back for. 1.7% of the heart's spawns are above the cap.

## Lake Viltri's hollow (tier 2, cap 25, band 13-19; on Route 2)

The lake is 41 blocks from Route 2, so it stays the path's: Magikarp (49%), Corphish (25%), Lotad (22%), Lombre and
a deep Chinchou in the water; Nincada (60%) and night Venonat (30%) on land, with Volbeat, Illumise, Chikorita and a
Squirtle on the shore (2-3% each). The heart is the hollow's far woods, a 144-block circle west of the lake, 10% of
the place and 201 blocks from the route: Bayleef become common there (22.5%, 16-19), and Ninjask (20-30) and Beedrill (19-30) buzz
through at 1% each. 0.9% of the heart's land spawns are above the cap.

## Frostpeak (tier 5, cap 40, band 28-38; off the path, 665 blocks from Route 4)

The mountain near (0, 0). On its lower slopes a player meets Absol (44%), Delibird and Snorunt (22% each), Eiscue
(10%) and, rarely, Larvitar and Pupitar (0.6% each), all inside 28-38. Above y180 -- the top ninth of the mountain,
11.1% of its area, rising to the summit at y310 -- the snow gets big: Piloswine (18%, 33-38) and Sneasel (2%), and
the presences, 1.1% each: Abomasnow (40-45), Mamoswine, Beartic and Avalugg (38-45), Glalie (42-45). The slopes'
Absol, Delibird and Snorunt still make up most of the summit's spawns (71%), so the climb is catchable; 4.1% of
what spawns up there is above the cap, to be caught on the next leg (cap 45).

## Marshy Marsh (tier 5, cap 40, band 28-38; near Route 6)

At the marsh's edges: Croagunk (36%), Stunfisk (30%), Toxicroak (24%, 37-38), Carnivine, Marshtomp and Swampert
(5%, 3%, 2%) and, in the rain, Goomy; in the water Croconaw (44%), Feraligatr (22%), Tympole, Palpitoad and Seismitoad.
The heart is a 176-block circle round the marsh's middle, 10.6% of the marsh and 193 blocks from the route.
Toxicroak and Seismitoad thicken there (22% each, catchable), and the presences come out: Swampert (38-45) and
Sliggoo (40-45) on the banks at 1.7% each, and a big Feraligatr (38-45) at 10% of the water spawns. Above the cap:
2.4% of the heart's land spawns, 6.35% of its water spawns.

## Lake Tilpey's open water (tier 7, cap 50, band 38-48; near Route 7)

Out on the lake, Gyarados (60%), Veluza (30%) and Golduck (10%), all 38-48, with Pelipper overhead. The heart is a
160-block circle round the middle of the lake, 10.2% of the open water (94.5% of it lake bed under y62). There the
Golduck share halves to make room for Gyarados and Wailord at 48-55 (2.5% each): 3.2% of the heart's water spawns
are above the cap of 50.

## The north Wedge, the dark forest (tier 6, cap 45, band 33-43; off the path, 457 blocks from Route 6)

Its outskirts are already a ghost wood: Haunter (44%), Drifblim (22%), Mismagius (13%), Misdreavus, Polteageist,
Sinistea, and the rare finds Spiritomb and Fuecoco's line (Crocalor, Skeledirge). The heart is the wood's deepest
point, a 96-block circle, 11% of the Wedge: Haunter and Drifblim at 38-43 crowd in (15% each), and among them Gengar,
Mismagius and Dusknoir at 43-50 (2% each). 3.8% of the heart's spawns are above the cap.

## The Great Crater (tier 8, cap 55, band 43-53; near Route 8)

On the slopes: Magmortar (49%, 48-53), Chandelure (17%), Charizard (10%, 43-53), Magmar, Litwick and Lampent, and a
rare Larvesta. The heart is the summit bowl: a 96-block circle round its floor (about y195-205, rim up to y302,
breached to the east), 4.8% of the place. Chandelure fill the bowl (19%, 51-53), and the bowl's presences are a
Charizard and a Magmortar at 53-60 and a Volcarona at 59-60 (2% each). 4.5% of the bowl's spawns are above the cap.

## The Rift's south-east arm (tier 9, cap 60, band 52-60; off the path, 209 blocks from Route 8)

The arm's floor is Victory Road's country: Claydol (49%), Golurk (25%), Gliscor (21%, 56-60), Gligar, and the finds
Druddigon and Bagon's Salamence (0.7% each). The heart is the arm's far end, a 128-block circle, 10.1% of the arm:
Salamence, Flygon and Haxorus at 60-62 (3.3% each). 6.7% of the heart's spawns are above the Victory Road cap of
60; 62 is the champion's top level, so they are the last things a player comes back for.
