# Encounter audit: what is live, per sub-region (2026-10-02)

**Status: measured, offline.** Every number here was produced by `tools/encounter_audit.py` on 2026-10-02 from the
compiled spawn pack, not from `data/spawns.json`'s intent. Nothing here was observed in a running game tonight.
The full per-table listing (193 tables: every species, chance, bucket, levels, stage, base-stat total and condition)
is regenerated by

```
python tools/compile_spawns.py
python tools/availability.py --write        # derived/availability.json: the leg each pool is first met on
python tools/encounter_audit.py --json derived/encounter_audit.json --md derived/encounter_audit.md
```

and for the pre-rebuild state, run the same at commit `2de7170` (PR #105's head, the data the owner played).

## What "live" means here, and why the compiled pack is it

- **The installed pack is the compiled pack.** The `cobblers_spawns` folder in `staging-2026-10-01`'s world
  datapacks (copied read-only under the coordination lock, 2026-10-02) is byte-identical to `tools/compile_spawns.py`
  run on `2de7170`: `--check` gives identical 164, different 0, missing 0.
- **Inside every sub-region, our tables are all there is.** `cobblers_suppress` (installed in the same world) is
  generated with `--subregions`: every inherited Cobblemon and COBBLEVERSE pool carries every sub-region polygon,
  route box and marine band as a coordinate anticondition. So the lakes' Magikarp are ours, not the pack's.
  The installed `cobblers_suppress` is dated 2026-09-28, before this rebuild; it is regenerated at install against
  the current `data/regions.json` (`reapply.py install`), and sub-region polygons are not changed by this rebuild.
- **Habitat Blocks replace the ambient pool inside their range** (EXP-021). Victory Road's 80 tiles carry the
  `vrc_*` pools; the 210 bird nest blocks and the mansion ward carry theirs.
- **A route corridor is a filtered copy of the sub-regions it crosses**: `compile_route` takes each crossed
  sub-region's entries and keeps only the route's `route_species_selection` (at most 20 species). The corridor is
  where a player who stays on the path spends most of their time.
- **Chances** are within a context (land, or water), with Cobbleverse's bucket weights (`best-spawner-config.json`:
  common 88.5, uncommon 10, rare 1.2, ultra-rare 0.3) renormalised over the buckets present. The renormalisation is
  an ASSUMPTION about Cobblemon 1.8.0; biome, time and depth conditions are listed, not modelled.

## The owner's observations, reproduced by name

**"Every lake is Magikarp, Goldeen, Barraskewda."** Magikarp and Goldeen are 54-100% of the water spawns in 11 of
the 14 lake and shore water tables. The most alike pairs share 99-100% of their water probability:
`tilpey_east_shore` = `tilpey_south_shore` (1.00), `tilpey_west_meadows` ~ both (0.99), `arrow_creeks` ~
`tilpey_west_meadows` (0.95). Across the 63 sub-region tables Magikarp and Goldeen are each ambient in 13, Gyarados
in 10, Barboach in 8. Barraskewda is ambient only in `tilpey_waters`; its pre-evolution Arrokuda is a third of
Arrow Lake's water. Where the path itself crosses water the corridor is worse: Route 1 over the River of Shrews and
Routes 2-3 over Lake Viltri spawn **only Surskit** on the water (the route selection filters the fish out).

**"Victory Road has Quagsire and Excadrill."** Every one of the six zones shares one filler block - Golbat,
Excadrill, Boldore, Graveler, Onix, Marowak, Swoobat, Durant and the rare Steelix/Crobat/Golem/Gigalith - which is
52% of a themed zone's land spawns and 90% of the plain `vrc_cave` tiles. Excadrill is 11-22% of every zone.
The Drowned Gallery's most common land spawn is Quagsire (29%); Whiscash shares its water. The zone prizes
(Milotic, Garchomp, Tangrowth, Dragapult, Metagross) are one species in the rare bucket of the core tiles only.

## Why: five causes in the data and its generator

1. **The evolution rule keeps evolved forms out.** `data/spawns.json` `evolution_policy.eligibility`: a stage is
   ambient "only when every threshold is at or below the local band minimum". With bands that start low (Northgate
   West is 25-45, so Forretress at 31 is out), 154 roster rows are weight 0 for "evolution requires level N > band
   min", and 91 more because the evolution is not by level. The rule is why a level-44 marsh still shows Wooper.
2. **Sixteen sub-regions carry a placeholder band of 25-45** whatever their leg, fifteen of them off the path: both Fungal Isle tables
   (nearest Route 1, cap 20), the Sunset, Northgate, Pine and Jungle isles, Frostpeak, the Wedge, both eastern dunes.
   Since 2026-09-28 a wild Pokemon over the thrower's cap cannot be caught (`data/level_cap.json`), so most of those
   spawns are uncatchable when the player first arrives. The Rift's arms are at 53-58, beside Routes 4 and 8.
3. **One water template, copied.** Magikarp/Goldeen (and often Gyarados, Seaking, Dondozo) were pasted into every
   painted lake on 2026-09-26 to fix lakes that spawned nothing; the lakes never got identities.
4. **The off-path rare slots hold nothing remarkable.** Off-path rare species are middle stages (Greedent, Dartrix,
   Whiscash) or nothing at all (14 of the 30 off-path tables have no rare entry). The only remarkable finds are Dratini
   at Shrew Lake and Relicanth at Arrow Lake, both at 1.2% of the water.
5. **Victory Road's tiles share one filler** (above), so the zones differ in their two or three theme species only.
